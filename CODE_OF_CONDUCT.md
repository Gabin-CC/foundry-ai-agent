# Code of Conduct

Foundry AI Agent is a hobby and open-source collaboration project. We want
issues, pull requests, reviews, and community discussions to be useful and
welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and
preferred languages.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Discuss technical choices and evidence without attacking people.
- Assume good intent, ask questions, and provide actionable feedback.
- Credit contributions and respect licenses and attribution.
- Protect other people's private information, credentials, and game data.
- Accept decisions to postpone or decline contributions without hostility.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory remarks.
- Personal insults, sustained trolling, or deliberately disruptive conduct.
- Publishing another person's personal information without consent.
- Sharing passwords, private campaign material, or other sensitive data.
- Retaliating against someone for raising a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to contributions and discussions in this repository and
to project-related community spaces, including cases where a contributor
publicly represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, temporarily
limit participation, or permanently ban repeated or serious violations.
Decisions will consider context, impact, and the safety of those involved.

For non-sensitive concerns, open a repository issue and state that you want
maintainer attention. For sensitive reports, contact a maintainer through a
private contact method listed on their GitHub profile; do not share personal
details in a public issue. Reports will be handled discreetly where possible.

This project follows these guidelines in addition to GitHub's applicable
platform policies.
